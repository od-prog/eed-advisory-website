# EED Advisory visual direction — draft for review

This is an original prototype direction, not an approved brand guide. The EED team should review the identity and all homepage copy before production use.

## Proposed direction

- **Palette:** deep evergreen `#103F3B`, evergreen `#155C55`, warm paper `#F5F4EC`, muted slate `#536560`, and restrained accents for the three areas: energy `#D78938`, water `#438FA0`, and climate `#6B8B57`.
- **Typography:** an editorial system serif for large headlines, paired with a system sans-serif for navigation and body copy. No remote font download is required.
- **Visual language:** orbital lines and layered fields suggest connected systems. The artwork is abstract and does not imply a specific project, place, client, or measured outcome.
- **Temporary mark:** a typographic E mark and wordmark are used in place of an approved logo. Replace them when EED supplies its logo and favicon.

## Copy status

The homepage hero and Energy, Water, and Climate descriptions are proposed drafts and are visibly labeled as such. They contain no impact figures, project examples, or organizational credentials.
